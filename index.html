How it works

Five steps from stranger to customer

A funnel is a short path that takes someone from seeing your business to raising their hand. Each step is built, connected and measured.

  1. Attract

    Ads, social posts, search or email send the right people to a page made for them.

  2. Capture

    A focused landing page with one clear offer and a short form that is easy to finish on a phone.

  3. Qualify

    A few smart questions sort serious buyers from browsers, so you spend time on the right leads.

  4. Route

    Each lead lands where it should within seconds: your inbox, phone, CRM, spreadsheet or a partner.

  5. Follow up

    Automatic emails or texts go out right away, and a simple dashboard shows what is working.
